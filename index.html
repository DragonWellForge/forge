Origin

Built by someone who needed it.

Dragon Well Forge started with two problems. A 13-year-old dog on cancer treatment with a care routine too complex to leave to chance. And a vet clinic paying $70 for a part that costs $1.50 to print.

"I don't want someone's negligence to cost my dog her life."

So we built the tools that didn't exist. A pet care page that lives on your wall. A fabrication shop that takes your concept seriously. Both are the same thing: turning something abstract — a worry, an idea, a need — into something real.

That's the forge. That's always been the forge.
