Origin

Built by a pet parent who needed it.

This started with Heidi, a 13-year-old Black Mouth Cur on cancer treatment. Her care is regimental, scheduled, and exact. Six medications. Three feeding windows. A chemo pill at 2000 that requires gloves.

One missed step isn't an inconvenience. It matters.

"I don't want someone's negligence to cost my dog her life."

So we built a pet care page that lives on your wall. Your sitter walks in, scans the QR code, and has everything they need — schedules, medications, quirks, commands — without a single text to dig through.

No app. No account. No "wait, which pill is the yellow one."

Dragon Well Forge exists because the stakes are real and the tools didn't exist. Now they do.

Process

From the well. Through the forge. Into the world.

01
Fill the Intake

Tell us about your animals. Schedules, medications, feeding instructions, commands, quirks. Everything that matters.

02
We Build Your Page

We create a clean, mobile-friendly care page hosted at your own private URL. Always accessible, always current.

03
Your Display Ships

A 3D printed QR display arrives at your door. Mount it by the entrance. Done.

04
Sitter Scans

They walk in, scan, and have everything they need. No calls, no texts, no guessing.
