Commission a Build

What do you need built?

You don't need a CAD file or a spec sheet. You need a problem and a willingness to describe it. The craftsman figures out the rest.

"Bring your concept. Leave with something real."

Community builds, vet clinics, shelters, and nonprofits are welcome to reach out about cost or donation options. The first community build was a Bair Hugger kennel adapter for a local vet — a $70 commercial part printed for $1.50 and given away for free. That's the spirit of the workshop.
