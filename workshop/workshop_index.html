The Philosophy

Walk in with a problem. Walk out with a solution.

In every fantasy world, the blacksmith's forge isn't a product catalog. It's a craftsman with capability. You describe what you need. They figure out how to make it.

"What do you need built?"

That's The Forge. The 3D printer is today's oven. The capability is the point. As tools grow, so does what we can make. Right now that means precision 3D printed parts, custom fixtures, functional prototypes, and community builds.

No idea too small. No problem too specific.

Origin Story

It started at a vet clinic.

At a routine oncology visit, a vet noticed a 3D printed ID tag and token of appreciation. They mentioned the heater adapters they use for sick animals — commercially sourced at $40–70 each.

The same part costs about $1.50 to print.

"This is what The Forge is for — solving real problems for the people and communities that matter."

The first community build was a Bair Hugger kennel adapter in PETG, printed for local vets at no cost. That's the spirit of The Forge. Precision made. Community focused. One layer at a time.
