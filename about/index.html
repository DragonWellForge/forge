The Current Crew

The ones who started all of this.

Dragon Well Forge exists because of these three. Their care, their personalities, and their needs shaped every product that comes out of this workshop.

Heidi
Forgemaster
13 years old · Black Mouth Cur · Cancer treatment

The reason Pet vCard exists. Heidi's care is regimental, scheduled, and exact — six medications, three feeding windows, and a chemo protocol that requires gloves. She is the proof of concept, the origin story, and the heart of this whole operation. Walk at her pace. Follow her lead.
