Why It Exists

Built for the pet parent who doesn't cut corners.

You know the schedule. You know which pill is which. You know that "just wing it" isn't an option when your animal is on a chemo protocol, an inhaler, or a six-medication regimen that has to be exact.

But you can't always be there.

"I don't want someone's negligence to cost my dog her life."

This started with Heidi — a 13-year-old Black Mouth Cur on cancer treatment. Her care is regimental, scheduled, and exact. Six medications. Three feeding windows. A chemo pill at 2000 that requires gloves. One missed step isn't an inconvenience. It matters.

Pet vCard exists because the stakes are real and the tools didn't exist. Now they do.
